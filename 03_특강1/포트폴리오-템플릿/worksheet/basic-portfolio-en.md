## Featured Project: CakGo

**Indoor everyday object finding assistant**  
October 13–22, 2026 · Four-person team · Team leader · Completed prototype

### Purpose

I wanted to help people who have visual difficulties find frequently used objects indoors. CakGo uses a phone camera to detect visible objects and provide Korean spoken directional guidance.

### My Contributions

- Planned the workflow, schedule, and task distribution.
- Fine-tuned YOLO and contributed to real-photo evaluation.
- Managed GitHub pull requests and merges, and supported integration.
- Prepared the demo access link and created the demonstration video.

### Approach and Outcome

The team compared YOLO and Grounding DINO and selected Grounding DINO with multi-prompt input as the final detector. The repository documents a comparison using 94 real phone photographs, including missed detections, false alarms, and limitations with distant objects and dim lighting.

The prototype connected camera input, object detection, visual output, and Korean voice guidance. We also tried YOLOE for description-based object search without training separately on personal items, but that experiment was unsuccessful. Speech-to-text for choosing an object was a planned addition and was not implemented.

### What I Learned

I learned to clarify the problem, motivation, and key terms before dividing tasks. Applying existing models also taught me the importance of testing in conditions that reflect the intended use. Teamwork required regular communication, clear priorities, and thorough preparation.

If I repeated the project, I would first survey intended users to identify the objects they struggle to find and the help they need. I would use those findings to prioritize detection targets and useful prototype features.

[Code repository](https://github.com/Jeminana/CakGo_AI-powered-indoor-object-finding-assistant) · [Real-photo evaluation report](https://github.com/Jeminana/CakGo_AI-powered-indoor-object-finding-assistant/blob/main/tests/results/real_photos/COMPARISON.md)
